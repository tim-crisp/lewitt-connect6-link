CONNECT 6 Link

Drag CONNECT 6 Link.app to Applications, eject this disk, then open the app from
Applications. Its controls appear in the menu bar, not the Dock.

Requires macOS 13 Ventura or newer on an Intel or Apple Silicon Mac, plus one
LEWITT CONNECT 6 running firmware 2.2.7. No particular serial number or USB port
is required. Unsupported firmware is refused rather than written to.

Linking starts off. Enable it when you want all three output volumes to follow.
Use the keyboard icon to select the output controlled by Mac volume keys.

If the release is described as unnotarized, macOS may block its first launch.
Only if you trust this project's download, use System Settings > Privacy &
Security > Open Anyway after attempting to open the app. Organization-managed
Macs may disallow this. Notarized releases do not need that exception.
